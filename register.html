PLMun QR Code–Based Visitor Monitoring and Attendance Management System

Summary: By registering, you agree to use the system responsibly, keep your QR code secure, and allow PLMun to collect and process your data for campus security and attendance purposes.
1. Purpose

This system replaces manual logbooks with a digital QR code–based check-in/check-out and attendance monitoring platform for PLMun students, faculty, staff, and visitors.

2. Account Responsibility

You must provide accurate information and keep your password and QR code confidential. You are responsible for all activity under your account.

3. Password Security

You must create a strong password (at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character). Weak passwords will be rejected.

4. QR Code Usage

Your QR code is your digital ID. Do not share or duplicate it. Report any lost or compromised QR code to PLMun administration immediately.

5. Data Privacy

PLMun collects your name, email, role, and attendance logs solely for identification, access control, and reporting. Your data is stored securely and is not shared with third parties without your consent or as required by law.

6. Prohibited Acts

Impersonation, sharing QR codes, proxy attendance, and tampering with the system are strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension or disciplinary action.
